3.5

Drill blocks

Days are built from named blocks, not drills picked on the fly.

Plyoblocks

Weighted-ball throws: positional control, then dynamic stability, then sequencing.

Patterning

Pre-throw prep with water bag, med ball, and bands.

Flowblocks

Momentum-based throws with more degrees of freedom.

Catch Play (CPP)

Structured catch-play progressions.

Long Toss

Extension and compression progressions.

Wattage

Underweight mound work: compressions, turn and burn, tornado, rhythm pulldown.

Mound Blend

Moves plyo work onto the mound, ending in an accuracy bullpen.

Rule

A block name means the same drill sequence every time it's programmed. If you change a block, you change it for every athlete who runs it.
