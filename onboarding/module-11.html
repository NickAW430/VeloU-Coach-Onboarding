Everfit

The plan you built in Modules 3-6 only matters if it reaches the athlete correctly and you can see whether he did it. This is where that happens.

11.1

Why this matters

A program that lives only in your head, or only on paper, is a program nobody can check. Everfit is the record.

Rule

If it isn't in Everfit, it didn't happen. Assign it, log it, and note it there, not in a text thread or a notebook.

11.2

What you need to know

Your senior coach walks you through each of these live, on your own account.

Setup
Profiles
Every athlete's profile is the source of truth: level, phase, program, and history. Check it before you check anything else.
Setup
Finding programs
Find the exact program, phase, and split from Modules 4 and 5, fast, without guessing at the code.
Setup
Assigning programs
Assign the right one to the right athlete. A wrong assignment is a wrong program on the floor tomorrow.
Substitutions
Making substitutions
Swap an exercise without breaking the phase's intent. If you're not sure it's an equal swap, ask before you make it.
On the floor
Logging workouts
Log what actually happened, not what was planned. That gap between planned and logged is a coaching note in itself.
On the floor
Exercise videos
Point an athlete to the demo before you re-explain a cue from scratch. It's faster and it's consistent.
Monitoring
Athlete compliance
Checking training compliance is part of knowing your athletes. A skipped session unlogged is worse than one flagged.
Monitoring
Falling behind
Following up when an athlete misses sessions, or compliance drops, is your job, not a system alert's.
Monitoring
Training history
Review history before you write a coach note. Know what he's already been through before you decide what's next.
Communication
Coach notes
Write the note the next coach needs, not the one that's fastest to type.
Communication
Athlete communication
Know what's fine to say to the athlete directly in-app, and what belongs in a call instead (Module 10.7).
11.3

Prove it: program a hypothetical athlete

Everything up to now was reading. This is doing it, on your own account, with your senior coach watching.

1
Get the situation

Your senior coach gives you a hypothetical athlete and a starting point: his level, phase, and any flags.

2
Build the 4-week plan

Program him in Everfit yourself, on your account, for the full four weeks.

3
Write your adjustment notes

Anywhere you moved off the general program, write down what you changed and why.

4
Walk it through

Explain your reasoning to your senior coach before you submit.
