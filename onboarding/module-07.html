7.3

The movement screen

Station 1. Height, weight, and H:W, then seven exams in this order. Each one confirms or questions what you saw in the one before.

45°Hip IR, each leg
60°+Adductor abduction
45°T-spine rotation, dominant side
~180°Shoulder ER + IR total arc
Rule

The screen is a chain of evidence. Never call a finding from one exam alone. Confirm it with the next one or with a manual test.

7.6

When to stop a test

A number is never worth an athlete. Stop, record why, and tell your Senior Director.

Pain

Any pain, anywhere. Stop the test and the session's remaining tests on that area. The family hears about it the same day.

Compensation

He can't hold the position the test requires. The number would measure the wrong thing.

Fatigue

Output is dropping trial to trial. Later numbers would understate him.

7.7

After the test

Enter it now

Numbers go into the system before the athlete leaves. Nothing lives on a notepad.

Check it

Does it make sense next to his other numbers and his last test? A strange number gets retested, not explained away.

Flag it

A Delta above +5 or a big drop from last time goes to your Senior Director the same day.

Retest

Re-diagnosis at the end of every block (Module 4). TBC Energy component retest every 2 weeks.
