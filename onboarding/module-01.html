1.3

The VeloU coach

What sets us apart

Relentless, prepared problem solving

You hunt for problems that need deep solutions, and you have the autonomy to test your theories. It never looks haphazard.

The requirement

Mudita

Genuine joy in someone else's success. Every great coach has it. Here it is the floor, not the ceiling.

Curiosity+Preparation=Autonomy
Curiosity−Preparation=Guessing

Same standard for a 13-year-old and a pro. Only the conversation outside of baseball changes, because the goal is a deep relationship with every athlete.

1.4

The five standards

You are held to these, and you hold everyone else to them.

Mudita

The vicarious joy for the success of others.

Relentlessly curious

Stop learning and complacency starts. Adjust theories, test, build from what you find, and do it fast.

Accountability

Own who you say you are and who you want to become. Hold others to the same bar, fearlessly.

Preparation

Preparation brings calm, and calm lets you see what others miss.

Empathetic

Before you judge, feel what the athlete, parent, or coach is feeling right now.
