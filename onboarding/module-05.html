5.1

Two separate decisions

Every strength program is a phase and a split. They never influence each other.

PhaseHis capacitySet by force plate diagnosis

Sets the load band and how each rep is executed (Module 4).

SplitHis throwingSet by the throwing calendar

Sets how many days he lifts, how much volume, and which vehicles.

Rule of thumb

Pick the split for the throwing period right now. Pick the phase for his capacity right now. Track the throwing calendar, not his lifting progress.

Exception

Movement Literacy splits are phase-locked. On ML, a phase change is a split change, because the shape of the week changes with the phase.

5.4

The Friday Primer

Appears on every non-axial split.

It isSpeed, not fatigue

An overcoming-iso hold superset into a light eccentric-tempo touch, with a short, light prep in front. Fewer rounds, long recovery.

It is notA lift day

Never add volume to it. If an athlete wants more on Friday, the answer is no.

5.7

It runs both ways

Throwing upLifting steps down

Follow the splits left to right as the throwing period advances. Move back as it resets.

Weight room emphasisThrowing steps down

When the weight room is the priority, throwing may drop to 1 day a week. Keep at least 2 cardio days a week, set from VO2 max.

Rule

Split changes happen at the re-diagnosis point, like every other block decision. The throwing calendar tells you which split the next block should be.
