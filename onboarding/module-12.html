Monday.com

The athlete pipeline lives here, not in your head and not in a text thread. If you didn't update it, it didn't happen.

12.1

Why this matters

An athlete moves through stages before he ever gets to your floor, and other people need to see exactly where he is without asking you.

Rule

If someone else has to text you to find out where an athlete stands, the board wasn't updated.

12.2

What you need to know

Your senior coach walks you through the board live, on a real account.

Pipeline
The pipeline
Understand every stage an athlete passes through, from first contact to on the floor. Know:
  • How an athlete actually arrives here, and the process behind it
  • What automations run on their own, without a coach touching anything
  • The point where you need to start paying attention to him
  • How and why an athlete ends up assigned to you specifically
Pipeline
New athlete onboarding
Know the order a new athlete moves through:
  • Introduced to the building
  • Introduced to training
  • Moved through the weight room
  • Introduced to the other coaches
  • Handed to you, his primary coach
Status
Evaluation status
Know:
  • Where and how to see the notes from his evaluation
  • How to interpret what they say
  • How to actually use that information once you have it
Status
Programming status
Know how to adjust his status in Monday.com so it stays current with what's actually happened in Everfit (Module 11).
Notes
Athlete notes
Know where and how to reference or leave a note on an athlete, including his goals, injury history, and where he sits in his training cycle.
Notes
Documenting conversations
A conversation that mattered gets written down here, not just remembered.
Ownership
Tasks and responsibilities
Know where and how to reference your tasks and responsibilities in Monday.com, including what's assigned to you and what you're handing off to someone else.
Ownership
Internal communication
Know how to use Monday.com to communicate with other coaches and with management, so the right person sees a concern instead of just whoever you happened to run into.
Upkeep
Updating athlete information
Stale information is worse than no information. Know:
  • When something needs to change
  • How, and what exactly, to change
  • How you get notified something changed
  • Who you need to notify when you're the one who changed it
Upkeep
Daily and weekly
Know exactly how often each kind of update needs to happen, so nothing gets missed on a daily or a weekly pass.
Ownership

An accurate board is how you create ownership over an athlete's experience: everyone touching him sees the same real picture, not a version filtered through you.

12.3

Prove it: run a hypothetical athlete through the board

With your senior coach, add a hypothetical athlete to Monday.com and work through real scenarios.

1
Add the athlete

Create the card and move him into the pipeline at the right stage.

2
Evaluation complete

Update his evaluation status, and write the note the next coach needs from it.

3
Programmed

Move his programming status once he's in Everfit (Module 11), and flag it if compliance drops.

4
A missed session

Log the follow-up and document the conversation, not just the fact that it happened.
