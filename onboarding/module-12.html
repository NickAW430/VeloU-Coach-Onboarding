Monday.com

The athlete pipeline lives here, not in your head and not in a text thread. If you didn't update it, it didn't happen.

12.1

Why this matters

An athlete moves through stages before he ever gets to your floor, and other people need to see exactly where he is without asking you.

Rule

If someone else has to text you to find out where an athlete stands, the board wasn't updated.

12.2

What you need to know

Your senior coach walks you through the board live, on a real account.

Pipeline
The pipeline
Understand every stage an athlete passes through, from first contact to on the floor.
Pipeline
New athlete onboarding
Know what has to happen, and in what order, before a new athlete's first session.
Status
Evaluation status
Where he is in evaluation, from booked to reported (Module 2, Module 7).
Status
Programming status
Whether he's programmed, and whether that program matches what the evaluation actually said.
Notes
Athlete notes
Write what the next person touching this athlete needs to know, including his goals, injury history, and where he sits in his training cycle.
Notes
Documenting conversations
A conversation that mattered gets written down here, not just remembered.
Ownership
Tasks and responsibilities
Know what's assigned to you, and what you're handing off to someone else.
Ownership
Internal communication
Communicate a concern here so the right person sees it, not just whoever you happened to run into.
Upkeep
Updating athlete information
Keep the record current. Stale information is worse than no information.
Upkeep
Daily and weekly
Know exactly what needs an update today versus what's a weekly pass, so nothing gets missed on either cadence.
Ownership

An accurate board is how you create ownership over an athlete's experience: everyone touching him sees the same real picture, not a version filtered through you.

12.3

Prove it: run a hypothetical athlete through the board

With your senior coach, add a hypothetical athlete to Monday.com and work through real scenarios.

1
Add the athlete

Create the card and move him into the pipeline at the right stage.

2
Evaluation complete

Update his evaluation status, and write the note the next coach needs from it.

3
Programmed

Move his programming status once he's in Everfit (Module 11), and flag it if compliance drops.

4
A missed session

Log the follow-up and document the conversation, not just the fact that it happened.
