6.4

Where to spend his time

Placement picks the program. These rules decide what matters most inside it.

At or below velo norm→Energy Production first. Build the engine.
Above velo norm→Lowest-hanging fruit. Attack his weakest component.
Energy Production 97+→Shift to transference. The engine isn't the limiter.
Negative Delta · Production above velo→Diminishing weight room returns. Shift to skill and transference.
Negative Delta · Production below velo→Weight room gains still available. Keep building.

Transference patterns

Read the med balls against the kneeling throw.

High kneeling · low velo→Ground-to-pelvis problem. Then check: production or mechanical?
1 lb above norm · 2 lb at norm→Mechanical leak from the pelvis.
Low kneeling in a pro→Hip clearance issue.
Rule

Fit the model around the athlete, never the athlete into the model. If the numbers and your eyes disagree, bring both to your Senior Coach.
