10.1

The value gap

The work doesn't stop being valuable. It stops being visible.

Illustration, not measured data
75%+of families sign on after their evaluation. They see it.
~24%renewal rate by revenue. Most don't stay.
86¢of every new recurring dollar just replaces a family we lost.
Why

We cost more than the average facility and deliver significantly more. When families stop seeing that difference, especially after their son has a bad game, the price is all that's left.

10.4

"He threw badly on Saturday"

Getting better in the building doesn't automatically show up in a game. Transfer takes time. Explain that before it sounds like an excuse.

1Acknowledge

Empathy first. It was a hard day for him and for them.

2Separate

Training capacity and game performance are different things. Transfer takes time.

3Show

The numbers that are moving. Real data, not reassurance.

4Next step

What we're doing about it, and when they'll hear from you again.

Example. Make it yours, keep the structure. "I saw Saturday was rough, and I know that's frustrating for all of you. What we build in here doesn't show up in a game on a straight line. His body has to learn to use it under pressure, and that takes time. Here's what we know is moving: his Energy Production is up since his last test and his kneeling velo is climbing. This week we're adding more mound work so it starts transferring. I'll check in with you after his next outing." Numbers in the example are placeholders. Use his.
10.5

The retest review

Three questions, every time.

1What changed?

Compare this report to the last one. Lead with the component that moved most.

2What does it mean?

Put it in the energy chain. What's getting stronger, and what's still limiting him?

3What's next?

The next block, the next phase, and the date of the next retest.
