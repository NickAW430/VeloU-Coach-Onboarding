8.3

Velocity-based training

When a lift is VBT-tracked, the bar speed is the prescription. Coach the speed, not a count.

Force Control

VBT sits on the eccentric. It enforces the speed of the drop.

Acceleration

VBT sits on the concentric. Speed off the pins or out of the pause.

Applied Power

Hit the zone on every cluster, not just the first one.

The "+1"

AMRAP finisher on the day's two trained patterns only. Never accessories.

Stop the set

The moment bar speed falls out of the zone, or sprint and jump quality drops, the set is over. More reps at the wrong speed train the wrong thing.

8.4

Never run these short-staffed

TL P2 · Supra-max eccentric

105-125% of 1RM, caught on pins, pendulum, rack, or machine. A coach or partner resets the load every rep. If the supervision isn't right, the athlete stays in P1.

FC P3 · Eccentric power

88-94%, near-max reps released to pins with a 2-person reset. Needs a coach and a setup that can manage it safely.

Sled push

Force Control's extremely heavy sled push climbs 75%, 100%, then 125% of body mass over 10 yards. It sits before a Lunge/Posterior day. Slow, heavy pushes; load it correctly.
