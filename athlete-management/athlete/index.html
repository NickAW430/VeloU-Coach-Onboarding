Energy Interference · Arm Strength

SCORE{{ ei }}

Think of your shoulder, elbow and forearm like a filter--if it's not strong enough to handle what your body produces, you're either going to get hurt or hit a wall in performance. We measure internal/external rotation strength, scaption and grip using the ArmCare dynamometer. Then we calculate your Arm Score (how strong your arm is relative to your bodyweight). If your score is below 85%, it tells us your shoulder needs to be stronger or that you may be fatigued. This directly affects how much throwing you can handle and how hard you can train. Arm care programs are tailored based on any relative or absolute weaknesses from the 4 tests.

Energy Transference · Rotational Capacity

SCORE{{ et }}

Throwing is all about transferring energy from the ground through body and into the baseball. We test that through our medicine ball tests and stationary throwing tests. If your medicine ball throw velocity is low, we are likely looking at a sequencing issue--your body isn't connecting the chain efficiently. If your kneeling throw is low, it likely means your arm speed or torso to shoulder connection needs more attention. These tests help us to figure out where your energy is leaking and how to fix it.
